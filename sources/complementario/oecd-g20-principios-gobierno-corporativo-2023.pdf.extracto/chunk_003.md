The G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance are set out in the Appendix to the OECD Recommendation on
Principles of Corporate Governance [OECD/LEGAL/0413] adopted by the OECD Council on 8 July 2015 and revised on
8 June 2023. The Principles were endorsed by the G20 in September 2023. For access to the official text of the
Recommendation, as well as other related information, please consult the Compendium of OECD Legal Instruments at
https://legalinstruments.oecd.org.
