G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance
The G20/OECD Principles of Corporate Governance help policy makers evaluate and improve the legal, 
regulatory and institutional framework for corporate governance. They identify the key building blocks 
for a sound corporate governance framework and offer practical guidance for implementation at the national 
level. The Principles also provide guidance for stock exchanges, investors, corporations and others that have 
a role in developing good corporate governance.
